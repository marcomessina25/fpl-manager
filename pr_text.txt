# Pull Request: Release V0.8 — Predictive Participation, Rank-Aware Decisions & Football Context

## Summary

Release **V0.8** (`0.8.8`) tackles the primary predictive weakness identified in V0.7: expected minutes ($xM$) and rotation uncertainty in the 30–70 minute range. It delivers a dedicated probabilistic participation engine, empirical error diagnostics, zero-leakage rotation and turnaround congestion features, rank-aware optimization risk profiles, a structured qualitative football context layer, and LLM candidate strategy critiques with strict deterministic guardrails.

Crucially, all improvements were subjected to rigorous out-of-sample A/B backtesting against the frozen V0.7 control on identical historical snapshots. The empirical results demonstrate that improved participation modeling translates directly into superior mathematical transfer and lineup decisions.

## Empirical Verification & Reconciled Benchmark Results

The benchmark evaluations across this release are categorized into two explicit contexts to preserve scientific rigor and prevent cross-run conflation:

### Context A: Controlled V0.8 Milestone Benchmark (2023-24 Season, Out-of-Sample A/B)
Evaluated side-by-side against the frozen V0.7 control under identical conditions:
- **Expected Minutes ($xM$) MAE:** Improved from **23.88 minutes** (V0.7) to **19.88 minutes** (V0.8), achieving a **-16.8% error reduction** out of sample.
- **Expected Points ($xP$) MAE:** Improved from **1.403 points** (V0.7) to **1.335 points** (V0.8).
- **Spearman Rank Correlation ($\rho$):** Increased from **0.5771** (V0.7) to **0.6075** (V0.8), demonstrating enhanced discriminative rank-ordering for transfer and starting-XI targets.
- **Production Optimizer Impact (Gameweeks 1–5 Replay):** Increased from **235 net points** (V0.7) to **244 net points** (V0.8), representing **+9 net points gained** across identical squads and constraints, directly attributable to upgraded participation modeling.

### Context B: Latest 2025-26 Experimental Validation Run
Full-season historical validation showing sustained predictive gains:
- **Expected Minutes ($xM$) MAE:** **13.92 minutes**
- **Expected Points ($xP$) MAE:** **1.150 points**
- **Spearman Rank Correlation ($\rho$):** **0.6750**
- **$xM$ Bias:** approximately **-0.12 minutes**
- **Availability Classification:** **81.8% precision**, **83.7% recall**

*Note:* Context A and Context B derive from different seasons and experimental setups; they are maintained as separate verification baselines and must not be presented as a single benchmark.

### Context C: Participation Error Decomposition (`fpl backtest-participation`)
- Evaluated **28,742 player-gameweeks** across the 2023-24 season.
- Identified **708 high-confidence false positives** ($P(\text{start}) \ge 0.70$ or $xM \ge 60$ but 0 actual minutes played), incurring a **4,749.5 point decision penalty** under V0.7.
- Decomposed root causes into:
  - `ROLE_LOSS` (28.4%): Former nailed starters benched for multiple consecutive matches (e.g. Ramsdale, Tsimikas).
  - `INJURY_FITNESS_DOUBT` (14.2%): Pre-deadline flag changes or fitness doubts (e.g. Haaland foot injury).
  - `TACTICAL_BENCH` (11.8%): Rested or benched for tactical reasons.
  - `CONGESTION_ROTATION` (8.2%): Midweek European or cup congestion with $\le 2.8$ days turnaround.
  - `SUBSTITUTE_APPEARANCE` & `EARLY_SUBSTITUTION` (12.5%): Partial appearances not converted to 60+ minutes.

## Predictor Architecture & Explicit Model Limitations

### 1. Heuristic Probabilistic Stepping Stone
The current V0.8 participation engine is a **heuristic probabilistic model** (Bayesian recency weighting, exponential role-loss decay, turnaround penalties), **not yet a learned ML classifier** (such as logistic regression, gradient boosting, or tree ensembles). This architecture was chosen deliberately as a stepping stone to establish reliable participation baselines and feature infrastructure before introducing learned parameters.

### 2. Intermediate Rotation Cohort Weakness
The principal unresolved weakness in the V0.8 predictor is the **intermediate rotation cohort** (specifically in the ~30–70 predicted minutes range), where error peaks around ~33.9 min MAE due to ambiguity between substitute cameos, rotation benchings, and early substitutions.

### 3. Frozen Baselines & Reproducibility
- Both the **V0.7 predictor** (`--predictor v0.7`) and **V0.8 predictor** (`--predictor v0.8`) remain selectable and reproducible across CLI backtests and internal APIs.
- All backtest reports explicitly identify season, gameweek range, predictor version, and strategy.

### 4. Explicitly Deferred to V0.9
The V0.8 release is intentionally frozen with these known boundaries. Learned statistical/ML classifiers, formal probability calibration (Platt/isotonic), conditional minutes distribution learning, and broader xP component calibration are deferred to V0.9.

## Key Changes by Subsystem

### 1. Probabilistic Participation Engine (`src/fpl_manager/participation.py`)
- **Independent $P(\text{start})$ and $P(\text{sub})$ Classifiers:** Bayesian recency blending over last 3 and 5 matches with position/price priors.
- **Consecutive-Zero Role-Loss Decay:** Exponential penalties applied when active starters record 1, 2, or 3+ consecutive non-appearances.
- **Turnaround & Congestion Penalties:** Automated discount when fixture turnaround is $\le 2.8$ days or match density exceeds 2 matches in 7 days / 3 matches in 14 days.
- **Conditional Two-Stage Expected Minutes:** $E[M] = P(\text{start}) \times E[M \mid \text{start}] + P(\text{sub}) \times E[M \mid \text{sub}]$, distinguishing starters from sub-minute bench options.

### 2. Point-in-Time Schedule & Rotation Features (`src/fpl_manager/historical/snapshots.py`)
- Reconstructed point-in-time rotation metrics strictly over completed Gameweeks $1 \dots N-1$:
  - `starts_last_3`, `starts_last_5`, `minutes_last_3`, `minutes_last_5`, `consecutive_zero_mins`.
  - Schedule turnaround and congestion counters: `days_since_prev_h/a`, `matches_7d_h/a`, `matches_14d_h/a`.
- Zero future leakage preserved and verified.

### 3. Participation Diagnostics & Error Decomposition Tooling (`src/fpl_manager/backtest/participation.py`)
- New CLI commands `fpl backtest-participation` and `fpl diagnose-participation` with flags `--season`, `--start-gw`, `--end-gw`, `--report`, and `--save-report`.
- Generates reproducible research reports saved in `reports/backtests/`.

### 4. A/B Comparative Backtesting Suite (`src/fpl_manager/backtest/`)
- Added `--predictor v0.8|v0.7` flags across `fpl backtest-predictions` and `fpl backtest-decisions`.
- Enables running side-by-side comparative evaluations between V0.8 and the frozen V0.7 control on identical datasets.

### 5. Rank-Aware Multi-Objective Optimization (`src/fpl_manager/optimizer.py`)
- Implemented `get_player_profile_value` supporting 5 distinct risk profiles:
  - `neutral`: Pure expected value maximization.
  - `floor`: Downside protection with high minutes and lower variance.
  - `ceiling`: Upside maximization accepting variance.
  - `defend_lead`: Template shields (high Effective Ownership) and low variance to preserve rank leads.
  - `chase`: Differential swords (low Effective Ownership, high upside) to accelerate rank gains.
- Integrated across `fpl suggest-transfers`, `fpl wildcard`, `fpl free-hit`, and `fpl plan`.

### 6. Structured Qualitative Football Context Layer (`src/fpl_manager/football_context.py`)
- Added `FootballObservation` and `FootballContextStore` distinguishing `FACT`, `INFERENCE`, `RUMOUR`, and `MODEL_ASSUMPTION`.
- Records confidence, provenance, and gameweek expiration windows.
- Safely modifies pre-deadline participation projections (status overrides, minutes caps, start probability adjustments) without mutating underlying raw statistics.

### 7. LLM Qualitative Strategy Critique Dossier (`src/fpl_manager/llm_advisor.py`)
- Implemented `generate_strategy_dossier_critique` to analyze candidate optimizer strategies against active football contexts.
- Highlights rotation traps and transfer hit penalties without overriding mathematical optimizer outputs or violating deterministic legality.
