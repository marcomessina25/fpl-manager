## 1. Executive Summary

V1.1 delivered a mathematically sound strategic squad construction framework, a dedicated Studio GUI, and reproducible multi-season evaluation. However, empirical analysis of multi-season walk-forward runs and live management scenarios identified two critical operational gaps:

1. **The Dead-Capital Departure Trap:** When players are transferred, loaned, or released outside the Premier League (e.g. during summer or January transfer windows), naive expectation algorithms often treat them as merely having zero short-term expected points ($0.0\text{ xP}$). In the optimizer, an unplayed bench asset or an underperforming starter with low minutes may be prioritized for substitution or transfer over a departed player, locking up millions in unrecoverable "dead capital". On the buy side, unless departure status is strictly recognized, stale snapshot caches can suggest acquiring departed players.
2. **Uncalibrated Seasonal Chip Utilization:** In previous backtest versions, chip evaluation was either disabled or evaluated locally per-gameweek. In the official FPL game, managers receive two sets of chips:
   - **First Half (GW 1–19):** $1\times$ Wildcard, $1\times$ Free Hit, $1\times$ Triple Captain, $1\times$ Bench Boost.
   - **Second Half (GW 20–38):** $1\times$ Wildcard, $1\times$ Free Hit, $1\times$ Triple Captain, $1\times$ Bench Boost.
   Without historical calibration, naive optimizers commit pathological timing errors—such as burning a Wildcard in GW2 immediately after picking an "ideal" strategic squad in GW1, or using Bench Boost on unconfirmed non-double gameweeks.

## 2. Problem Statement & Product Thesis

### 2.1 The Premier League Departure Problem

In fantasy football, a player transferred out of the league is fundamentally different from a bench asset or an injured player:
- An injured player (`status = 'i'`) will eventually return and retains recovery probability.
- A rotation player (`status = 'a'`, low minutes) provides contingency coverage and price preservation.
- A **departed player** (`status = 'u'` or removed from club rosters) produces **permanently zero points**, cannot play under any circumstances, loses market liquidity, and consumes a fixed squad slot.

#### Pathological Failure Mode in Prior Engines:
When evaluating transfers with a standard transfer limit (e.g. 1 Free Transfer), if a manager has:
- Player A: Midfielder sold to Saudi Pro League or La Liga (£8.0m, $0.0\text{ xP}$).
- Player B: Active Premier League defender playing against a top-4 team ($1.8\text{ xP}$).
A myopic single-GW solver might prioritize swapping Player B for a defender with $4.5\text{ xP}$ ($\Delta = +2.7\text{ xP}$), leaving Player A stranded on the bench indefinitely as dead capital.

#### V1.1.5 Invariant:
> **A player no longer in the Premier League represents permanent dead capital and must be assigned an emergency offload priority higher than any active, eligible Premier League player, regardless of fixture difficulty.**
> **On the buy side, no player with departure flags or zero future scheduled fixtures may ever appear in candidate pools.**

### 2.2 Historical Chip Timing & Calibration

A fantasy manager's season outcome is heavily influenced by chip deployment. FPL provides exactly 8 chip actions per season:

| Half | Gameweek Range | Wildcard | Free Hit | Triple Captain | Bench Boost |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Window 1** | GW 1 – GW 19 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| **Window 2** | GW 20 – GW 38 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

*(Unused Window 1 chips expire at the GW19 deadline and do not roll over into Window 2).*

#### The Anti-Pathology Imperative:
1. **The GW2 Wildcard Fallacy:** If the engine constructs a strategic squad in GW1 optimized over a 5-gameweek horizon, it must not trigger a Wildcard in GW2 unless a catastrophic black-swan event occurs ($\ge 3$ long-term injuries or departures). Triggering early Wildcards due to minor 1-week xP fluctuations destroys long-term optionality.
2. **Double Gameweek (DGW) Synergy:** Triple Captain and Bench Boost have significantly higher expected returns during confirmed Double Gameweeks (where starters play 180 minutes). The engine must identify and reserve chips for high-density fixture periods.
3. **Blank Gameweek (BGW) Cushioning:** Free Hit achieves maximum leverage when domestic cup clashes leave fewer than 6–8 active fixtures in a gameweek.

## 3. Detailed Technical Architecture

### 3.1 Pillar 1: Premier League Departure & Dead Capital Engine

#### 3.1.1 Detection Specification
A player is identified as `DEPARTED_LEAGUE` if any of the following conditions hold in the point-in-time snapshot:
1. `status == "u"` (Official FPL Unavailable / Transferred flag).
2. `chance_of_playing_this_round == 0` AND `chance_of_playing_next_round == 0` with transfer/loan news (e.g. `"Transferred to..."`, `"Joined..."`, `"Loaned to..."`, `"Contract terminated"`).
3. The player's club ID does not map to an active Premier League team, or the player has 0 scheduled fixtures remaining in the season schedule (`fixtures_remaining == 0` while the season is ongoing).

```python
@dataclass(frozen=True, slots=True)
class PlayerEligibilityStatus:
    player_id: int
    is_in_premier_league: bool
    status_code: str
    news: str
    dead_capital_penalty: float
```

#### 3.1.2 Priority Sell Offloading Mechanism
In transfer optimization (`decide_transfers`), departed players receive an explicit structural penalty in the objective function:
$$\text{Objective}(\text{transfer\_out}) = \Delta\text{xP} + \text{DeadCapitalWeight} \cdot \mathbb{I}_{\text{departed}}(p_{\text{out}}) \cdot \frac{\text{price\_tenths}(p_{\text{out}})}{10.0}$$
where `DeadCapitalWeight` ($\ge 10.0$) ensures that recovering tied-up funds from departed players takes absolute priority over swapping active underperformers.

#### 3.1.3 Strict Buy-Side Filtering
In `generate_strategic_candidates`, `suggest_transfers`, and `suggest_wildcard`:
```python
eligible_pool = [
    p for p in candidate_pool
    if p.status != "u"
    and not is_departed_from_premier_league(p)
    and p.id not in departed_player_ids
]
```
No departed player can ever be selected in initial squads, transfers, Wildcards, or Free Hits.

### 3.2 Pillar 2: Historically Calibrated Seasonal Chip Strategy Engine

#### 3.2.1 Two-Window Chip Quota Tracking
The state engine tracks chip inventory partitioned by gameweek window:

```python
@dataclass
class SeasonalChipInventory:
    # Window 1 (GW 1–19)
    wildcard_w1: bool = True
    free_hit_w1: bool = True
    triple_captain_w1: bool = True
    bench_boost_w1: bool = True

    # Window 2 (GW 20–38)
    wildcard_w2: bool = True
    free_hit_w2: bool = True
    triple_captain_w2: bool = True
    bench_boost_w2: bool = True

    def available_chips(self, gameweek: int) -> set[str]:
        if 1 <= gameweek <= 19:
            chips = set()
            if self.wildcard_w1: chips.add("wildcard")
            if self.free_hit_w1: chips.add("free_hit")
            if self.triple_captain_w1: chips.add("triple_captain")
            if self.bench_boost_w1: chips.add("bench_boost")
            return chips
        elif 20 <= gameweek <= 38:
            chips = set()
            if self.wildcard_w2: chips.add("wildcard")
            if self.free_hit_w2: chips.add("free_hit")
            if self.triple_captain_w2: chips.add("triple_captain")
            if self.bench_boost_w2: chips.add("bench_boost")
            return chips
        return set()
```

#### 3.2.2 Historically Grounded Trigger Conditions

| Chip | Window | Optimal Trigger Heuristic | Anti-Pathology Constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wildcard 1** | GW 1–19 | Squad degradation index $\ge 3.0$ (injuries + departures) OR major fixture swing in GW 6–10. | **Prohibited in GW 2–4** unless $\ge 3$ players permanently unavailable. |
| **Triple Captain 1** | GW 1–19 | Premium asset ($> £11.0\text{m}$) has a confirmed DGW or exceptionally high single-GW expected points ($\text{xP} \ge 11.5$). Forced play by GW 19 deadline. | Cannot trigger on rotation-risk assets ($< 80$ projected minutes per fixture). |
| **Bench Boost 1** | GW 1–19 | All 4 bench players have guaranteed starts ($\text{xM} \ge 60$) and fixture FDR $\le 3$. Forced play by GW 19. | Cannot trigger if bench contains $> 0$ zero-minute assets. |
| **Free Hit 1** | GW 1–19 | Blank Gameweek with $\le 6$ scheduled matches OR $\ge 5$ starters blanking. | Cannot trigger if starting XI has 11 playing starters. |
| **Wildcard 2** | GW 20–38 | Setup for major DGWs (typically GW 28–34) or post-AFCON/Asian Cup reconstruction. | Must preserve minimum 4 gameweeks buffer before end of season. |
| **Free Hit 2** | GW 20–38 | Major Spring Blank Gameweek (GW 29 / GW 33 FA Cup clashes). | Targeted strictly at maximum fixture-deficit gameweek. |
| **Triple Captain 2**| GW 20–38 | Major Spring Double Gameweek with elite captain asset (e.g. Haaland/Salah DGW). | Prioritizes highest cumulative DGW projected xP across remaining fixtures. |
| **Bench Boost 2** | GW 20–38 | Major Double Gameweek with 15 active playing fixtures across full squad. | Requires active bench expected points $\ge 16.0\text{ xP}$. |
