## 1. Executive Summary & Core Hypothesis

Throughout V0.9 to V1.2.5, FPL Manager's quantitative core relied on pure-Python, zero-dependency statistical models:
- **Participation:** Linear/L2-regularized logistic regression with PAVA isotonic calibration.
- **Minutes:** Heuristic conditional minutes expectations.
- **Points:** Analytical component formulations using coarse linear FDR multipliers ($1.0 + (3 - \text{FDR}) \times 0.10$).
- **Transfers & Chips:** Deterministic branch-and-bound optimization with fixed hurdles and rolling 3-GW discounted horizons.

While V1.2.5 achieved the lowest cross-season variance of any version ($\pm 116.9$) and reached **2,046.6 Track A** and **2,087.8 Track B**, it operated under strict linear/additive constraints. It could not capture complex non-linear interaction terms:
- Schedule congestion interacting with squad depth and player age.
- Team offensive strength interacting with opponent defensive vulnerabilities.
- Non-linear bonus point accrual dynamics.
- State-dependent chip deployment value over rolling fixture swings.

### The V1.3 Scope & Promotion Criterion
**V1.3 introduces a full Gradient Boosting Decision Tree (GBDT) engine across prediction, decision-making, and chip timing.**

> **Primary Decision Rule:** If V1.3 GBDT demonstrates superior performance over V1.2.5 in historical data across our 5-season benchmark ledger (`2021-22` through `2025-26`), **we will adapt the new optimizer into the live production version** (CLI, Web GUI, and LLM briefings).
> 
> Furthermore, whichever engine proves superior (hopefully V1.3) will become the default recommendation engine in the V1.4 Historical Simulation Sandbox.

### Pillar 1: GBDT Hierarchical Participation & Minutes Model
- **Classification (`P(start)` and `P(sub | not start)`):**
  - Implement `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` trained on historical appearance data.
  - Features: Rolling starts (3, 5, 10 matches), minutes volume, rest days since prior fixture, matches in 7/14 days (European congestion), player age, position, price tier, and manager stability.
  - Captures non-linear thresholds (e.g. drop in start likelihood when rest $\le 3$ days *only* for players aged $\ge 31$ or in teams with European commitments).
- **Conditional Minutes Regression ($E[M \mid \text{start}], E[M \mid \text{sub}]$):**
  - Implement `HistGradientBoostingRegressor` to predict expected minutes conditional on appearance, distinguishing 60-minute tactical substitutions from 90-minute full-match assets.

### Pillar 2: Non-Linear Attacking Threat, Clean Sheet & Bonus GBDT
- **Attacking Threat ($xG_{f}, xA_{f}$):**
  - Replace static linear FDR multiplier ($1.0 + (3 - \text{FDR}) \times 0.10$) with a learned model integrating:
    - Player rolling underlying stats ($xG_{90}, xA_{90}$, shots, key passes).
    - Opponent defensive conceding rate (rolling $xGC$, goals conceded home/away).
    - Team offensive efficiency and venue (Home / Away).
- **Clean Sheet Probability ($P(\text{CS})$):**
  - Non-linear probability estimator for defensive returns based on opponent attacking threat vs team defensive solidity.
- **Non-Linear Bonus Model:**
  - Estimating bonus point capture probability based on predicted attacking dominance and match clean sheet expectation.

### Pillar 3: GBDT Strategic Decisor & Chip Timing (`DecisionEngineV13`)
- **GBDT Chip Opportunity Evaluator:**
  - Instead of static heuristics (e.g. Free Hit when blank $\ge 4$ teams, Triple Captain when $xP \ge 8.0$), train a GBDT evaluator predicting the marginal seasonal gain of deploying a chip in gameweek $t$ vs holding it for future gameweeks.
- **Strategic Transfer Hurdle Adaptation:**
  - Dynamic hurdle adaptation: dynamically tuning the transfer hurdle based on squad fragility, upcoming DGW/BGW volatility, and bank flexibility.

### Pillar 4: Zero-Leakage Temporal Discipline & Optional Packaging
- **Temporal Walk-Forward Discipline:**
  - Models evaluated on target season $S$ are trained strictly on data prior to season $S$ (expanding window).
  - Explicit assertion ensuring no match facts from GW $t$ or later enter training sets for GW $t$.
- **Dependency Isolation & Graceful Fallback:**
  - Add optional dependency in `pyproject.toml`:
    ```toml
    [project.optional-dependencies]
    ml = ["scikit-learn>=1.4,<1.6", "numpy>=1.26,<2.2"]
    ```
  - If `scikit-learn` is installed, V1.3 GBDT runs as primary.
  - If `scikit-learn` is absent, the system gracefully falls back to the deterministic pure-Python standard-library engine (`DecisionEngineV125` / `LogisticModel`), maintaining zero-breakage for lightweight environments.

### Pillar 5: 5-Season Audited Benchmark Ledger (`reports/v13/`)
- Run reproducible full-season backtests across all 5 historical seasons:
  `2021-22`, `2022-23`, `2023-24`, `2024-25`, `2025-26`.
- Compare V0.9, V1.0, V1.1, V1.1.5, V1.2, V1.2.5, and V1.3 in dual-track format:
  - **Track A:** Pure transfer engine without chips.
  - **Track B:** Transfer engine with seasonal chip orchestration.
- **Production Adoption:** If Track A Mean Net $> 2,046.6$ or Track B Mean Net $> 2,087.8$ with acceptable variance, adapt `DecisionEngineV13` to live CLI, GUI, and briefings as the primary engine.
