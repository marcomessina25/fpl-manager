## 1. Executive Summary & Core Hypothesis

Throughout V0.9 to V1.2.5, FPL Manager's quantitative core relied on pure-Python, zero-dependency statistical models:
- **Participation:** Linear/L2-regularized logistic regression with PAVA isotonic calibration.
- **Minutes:** Heuristic conditional minutes expectations.
- **Points:** Analytical component formulations using coarse linear FDR multipliers ($1.0 + (3 - \text{FDR}) \times 0.10$).
- **Transfers & Chips:** Deterministic branch-and-bound optimization with fixed hurdles and rolling 3-GW discounted horizons.

While V1.2.5 achieved the lowest cross-season variance of any version ($\pm 116.9$) and reached **2,046.6 Track A** and **2,087.8 Track B**, it operated under strict linear/additive constraints. It could not capture complex non-linear interaction terms:
- Schedule congestion interacting with squad depth and player age.
- Team offensive strength interacting with opponent defensive vulnerabilities.
- Non-linear bonus point accrual dynamics.
- State-dependent chip deployment value over rolling fixture swings.

### The V1.3 Scope & Promotion Criterion
**V1.3 introduces a full Gradient Boosting Decision Tree (GBDT) engine across prediction, decision-making, and chip timing.**

> **Primary Decision Rule:** If V1.3 GBDT demonstrates superior performance over V1.2.5 in historical data across our 5-season benchmark ledger (`2021-22` through `2025-26`), **we will adapt the new optimizer into the live production version** (CLI, Web GUI, and LLM briefings).
> 
> Whichever engine proves superior across the 5 historical seasons will also become the default recommendation engine in the V1.4 Historical Simulation Sandbox.

### Pillar 1: GBDT Hierarchical Participation & Minutes Model
- **Classification (`P(start)` and `P(sub | not start)`):**
  - Implemented `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` trained on historical appearance data.
  - Features: Rolling starts (3, 5 matches), minutes volume, rest days since prior fixture, matches in 7/14 days (congestion), position, price tier, form, and ICT index.
  - Achieved **Val AUC: 0.9487** on out-of-sample validation data.
- **Conditional Minutes Regression ($E[M \mid \text{start}], E[M \mid \text{sub}]$):**
  - Implemented `HistGradientBoostingRegressor` to predict expected minutes conditional on appearance (**MAE: 7.93 min**).

### Pillar 2: Non-Linear Attacking Threat, Clean Sheet & Bonus GBDT
- **Attacking Threat ($xG, xA$):**
  - Integrated tabular regressors predicting player $xG$ and $xA$ from rolling form and opponent difficulty.
- **Clean Sheet Probability ($P(\text{CS})$):**
  - Probabilistic classifier estimating clean sheet probability conditional on opponent attacking threat and defensive solidity.

### Pillar 3: GBDT Strategic Decisor & Chip Timing (`DecisionEngineV13`)
- Implemented `DecisionEngineV13` inheriting multi-horizon optimization, candidate pool expansion, and role-specific transfer hurdles from `DecisionEngineV125`.
- Full registration in `fpl_manager.model_registry` and `fpl_manager.backtest.decision_engine`.

### Pillar 4: Zero-Leakage Temporal Discipline & Optional Packaging
- Zero-leakage temporal discipline maintained across all 5 seasons.
- Optional dependencies in `pyproject.toml` under `[project.optional-dependencies] ml = ["scikit-learn>=1.4,<1.7", "numpy>=1.26,<2.3"]`.
- Graceful, zero-error fallback to standard-library linear models when `scikit-learn` is absent.

### Pillar 5: 5-Season Audited Benchmark Ledger (`reports/v13/`)
- Complete 70-run benchmark executed across 5 seasons (`2021-22` to `2025-26`), 2 tracks (Track A & Track B), and 7 versions (V0.9, V1.0, V1.1, V1.1.5, V1.2, V1.2.5, V1.3).

### Key Empirical Findings:
1. **Outstanding Performance in Recent Season (2025-26):**
   - In 2025-26, V1.3 achieved the **highest score of any model**:
     - **Track A:** **2,144 pts** (+131 pts vs V1.2.5 at 2,013)
     - **Track B:** **2,169 pts** (+116 pts vs V1.2.5 at 2,053)
   - The GBDT models demonstrated superior sensitivity to the current Premier League tactical landscape.
2. **Historical Cross-Season Average:**
   - Over the full 5-season historical horizon, `DecisionEngineV125` remains stronger in multi-season mean net points (2,046.6 Track A, 2,087.8 Track B) with the lowest standard deviation (±116.9).
   - In earlier seasons (`2021-22`), the canonical GBDT model exhibited greater variance due to shifting league distributions.

### Primary Promotion Rule Verdict:
- Following the explicit rule: *"if results are better than 1.2.5 in historical data, we adapt the new optimizer in the live version"*:
  - **`DecisionEngineV125` remains the default engine for live production** (`suggest-transfers`, `lineup`, GUI, briefings), ensuring optimal stability.
  - **`DecisionEngineV13` is fully implemented and available** via `--decision-engine v1.3` / `--predictor v1.3` for users seeking advanced machine learning projections.
  - **V1.4 Default Engine:** `DecisionEngineV125` will serve as the default baseline in the V1.4 Historical Simulation Sandbox, with V1.3 available as an selectable option.
