## 1. Executive Summary & Core Hypothesis

Throughout V0.9 to V1.2.5, FPL Manager's quantitative core relied on pure-Python, zero-dependency statistical models:
- **Participation:** Linear/L2-regularized logistic regression with PAVA isotonic calibration.
- **Minutes:** Heuristic conditional minutes expectations.
- **Points:** Analytical component formulations using coarse linear FDR multipliers ($1.0 + (3 - \text{FDR}) \times 0.10$).
- **Transfers & Chips:** Deterministic branch-and-bound optimization with fixed hurdles and rolling 3-GW discounted horizons.

While V1.2.5 achieved the lowest cross-season variance of any version ($\pm 116.9$) and reached **2,046.6 Track A** and **2,087.8 Track B**, it operated under strict linear/additive constraints. It could not capture complex non-linear interaction terms:
- Schedule congestion interacting with squad depth and player age.
- Team offensive strength interacting with opponent defensive vulnerabilities.
- Non-linear bonus point accrual dynamics.

### The V1.3 Scope & Scientific Question
**V1.3 introduces a Gradient Boosting Decision Tree (GBDT) quantitative model as a challenger to the established baseline, evaluating its projections through the frozen V1.2.5 decision engine.**

> **Primary Scientific Question:** Does a more flexible quantitative predictor convert into better downstream FPL decisions under strict point-in-time historical evaluation?
> 
> **Promotion Criterion:** GBDT will only replace the canonical quantitative baseline if corrected, leakage-free, walk-forward out-of-sample evidence demonstrates superior decision-level performance across the multi-season benchmark. Otherwise, it will be retained as an experimental challenger (`--predictor v1.3`) or rejected.

### Pillar 1: GBDT Hierarchical Participation & Minutes Model
- **Classification (`P(start)` and `P(sub | not start)`):**
  - Implemented `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` trained on historical appearance data.
  - Features: Rolling starts (3, 5 matches), minutes volume, rest days since prior fixture, matches in 7/14 days (congestion), position, price tier, form, and ICT index.
  - Achieved **Val AUC: 0.9487** on out-of-sample validation data.
- **Conditional Minutes Regression ($E[M \mid \text{start}], E[M \mid \text{sub}]$):**
  - Implemented `HistGradientBoostingRegressor` to predict expected minutes conditional on appearance (**MAE: 7.93 min**).

### Pillar 2: Non-Linear Return Rate & Clean Sheet GBDT
- **Return Rates (Goal & Assist Per-90 Rates):**
  - Tabular regressors predicting player normalized goal and assist rates from rolling form, underlying rates, and opponent difficulty.
- **Clean Sheet Probability ($P(\text{CS})$):**
  - Probabilistic classifier estimating clean sheet probability conditional on opponent attacking threat and defensive solidity.

### Pillar 3: Frozen Optimizer Integration (`DecisionEngineV13`)
- `DecisionEngineV13` couples the GBDT quantitative predictor with the frozen V1.2.5 decision engine (candidate pool expansion, goalkeeper hurdles, rolling 3-GW discounted horizon).
- This strictly isolates the predictor effect from optimizer changes. Dedicated optimizer research is deferred to milestone V1.3.5.

### Pillar 4: Zero-Leakage Temporal Discipline & Optional Packaging
- Zero-leakage temporal discipline enforced across historical seasons.
- Optional dependencies in `pyproject.toml` under `[project.optional-dependencies] ml = ["scikit-learn>=1.4,<1.7", "numpy>=1.26,<2.3"]`.
- Fallbacks are explicit, non-silent in benchmark/research mode.

### Pillar 5: 5-Season Audited Benchmark Ledger (`reports/v13/`)
- Dual-track evaluation across 5 historical seasons (`2021-22` to `2025-26`).

### Key Scientific Findings & Control Ledger Reconciliation:
1. **Root Cause of V1.2.5 Control Value Variance (Reconciled):**
   - In an intermediate run, V1.2.5 scored 1,866 in 2022-23 and 1,964 in 2025-26.
   - **Root Cause:** When feature parity inputs (`days_since_prev_fixture`, `matches_last_7_days`, `matches_last_14_days`) were introduced for GBDT, they were inadvertently passed into `_get_forward_projections` and `load_historical_strategic_players` for all versions. For non-GBDT versions (which evaluate forward gameweeks via the V0.9 learned participation model), receiving the *current* deadline's turnaround caused future fixtures to be penalized with turnaround discounts inappropriately.
   - **Resolution:** Restricting turnaround and match frequency parameters to `is_gbdt` fully restored the exact V1.2.5 canonical control ledger (**2,046.6 Track A / 2,087.8 Track B; 1,917 in 2022-23 and 2,013 in 2025-26**).
2. **Primary Walk-Forward Evaluation ($N \ge 1$ Prior Seasons):**
   - Excluding the 2021-22 stress test, V1.3 achieves **2,032.8 pts (Track A)** and **2,078.8 pts (Track B)** with **+46.0 pts chip realization** (exceeding V1.2.5's +38.8 pts chip yield).
3. **Modern Data Scaling ($N \ge 2$ Prior Seasons):**
   - Across 2023–2026, GBDT decisively beats V1.2.5 by **+26.0 pts/season in Track A** (2,142.0 vs 2,116.0) and **+33.7 pts/season in Track B** (2,191.0 vs 2,157.3).

## 4. Final Milestone Verdict & Promotion Gate

Under the strict project promotion criteria:
- **Promotion to Default Production Baseline:** **REJECTED.** GBDT exhibits high sensitivity in sparse-data regimes (1,705 in 2022-23). `DecisionEngineV125` remains the active production baseline across all standard CLI, API, and Web Studio workflows due to superior aggregate stability (2,046.6 pts vs 1,941.6 pts across all 5 seasons, and 2,066.2 pts vs 2,032.8 pts across the 4 walk-forward seasons) and lower variance ($\pm 116.9$ vs $\pm 281.2$).
- **Challenger Retention:** **ACCEPTED.** V1.3 GBDT is retained as an official, leakage-free experimental challenger (`--predictor v1.3` / `--decision-engine v1.3`). Its proven superiority on dense data distributions ($N \ge 2$) establishes GBDT as a foundational component for future ensembling.
- **Frozen Baseline for V1.3.5 & V1.4:** **`DecisionEngineV125` is formally frozen** as the control engine for the upcoming V1.3.5 Optimizer Decision-Quality Study and V1.4 Historical Simulation Sandbox.
