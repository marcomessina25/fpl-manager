## 2. Root Cause Analysis

Gameweek-by-gameweek trace analysis of the 2023-24 and 2024-25 simulation trajectories identified four distinct architectural bottlenecks in V1.2's transfer and squad execution:

### 2.1 The Candidate Pool Funnel Bottleneck in `solve_transfers`
In `DecisionEngineV12.decide_transfers`:
```python
recs, _ = solve_transfers(
    num_transfers=k,
    squad_players=squad_opt,
    candidate_pool=cand_pool,
    ...,
    max_results=5,
)
```
1. `solve_transfers` scores and ranks candidate transfers using the **unweighted squad sum delta**:
   $$\Delta xP_{\text{squad}} = \sum_{p \in \text{in}} xP(p) - \sum_{p \in \text{out}} xP(p)$$
2. Only the top **5** recommendations from this unweighted search are returned.
3. Only *after* that pruning step does `DecisionEngineV12` re-evaluate those 5 moves using the asymmetric lineup objective `_evaluate_squad_lineup_xp(new_squad) - _evaluate_squad_lineup_xp(curr_squad)`.
4. **The pathology:** If a high-value Starting XI upgrade (e.g. replacing a starting midfielder for an explosive premium) is ranked 6th–20th in flat squad sum due to bench or budget permutations, `DecisionEngineV12` **never sees it**. High-leverage starting moves are discarded before lineup evaluation occurs.

### 2.2 Goalkeeper Transfer Churn & Wasted Free Transfers
In the 2023-24 replay, V1.2 spent **5 full free transfers** swapping goalkeepers:
* GW09: Jason Steele $\to$ Nick Pope
* GW11: Bart Verbruggen $\to$ Guglielmo Vicario
* GW17: Nick Pope $\to$ Ederson
* GW32: Ederson $\to$ Jordan Pickford
* GW38: Guglielmo Vicario $\to$ Ederson

Because goalkeepers are inexpensive (£4.5m–£5.5m) and fixture difficulty swings can generate clean-sheet projection deltas of $+1.0$ to $+1.5$ xP, the unconstrained 1-GW lineup evaluator views goalkeeper swaps as easy, high-efficiency gains. However, goalkeeper scoring has low upside and high variance, and spending 5 transfers on goalkeepers prevented V1.2 from acquiring explosive outfield assets during critical scoring windows (e.g. Richarlison's December hot streak, Cole Palmer's breakout, or Alexander Isak).

### 2.3 Single-Gameweek Myopia & Reactive Zigzagging
`_evaluate_squad_lineup_xp` evaluates transfers against the **immediate next gameweek only** ($H=1$). In 2023-24:
* In GW20, Haaland suffered a short foot injury; V1.2 sold Haaland for Ollie Watkins.
* In GW21, V1.2 sold Son for Mohamed Salah (who was departing for AFCON and suffered a hamstring injury), scoring 0 points.
* In GW23, V1.2 had to burn another transfer selling Salah for Richarlison.

Evaluating only 1 week ahead creates reactive panic selling on temporary 1-to-2 week absences and induces lateral zigzagging across fixtures, incurring massive opportunity costs compared to holding proven core premiums.

### 2.4 Suboptimal Bench Weighting During Chip Execution (Track B Deficit)
In Track B, V1.2 captures only **+24.4 pts** from chips across the season, whereas V1.0 captures **+59.6 pts** (+35.2 pt chip deficit):
* During **Bench Boost**, all 4 bench players contribute 100% of their points on the pitch, but V1.2 builds and manages the squad assuming bench points are worth only $0.15\times$.
* During **Free Hit**, the entire squad lasts for only 1 gameweek with zero bench rollover value; bench weight should be minimized ($0.05\times$) to concentrate maximum budget into the starting XI.
* V1.2 does not dynamically adapt `bench_weight` to the active chip context.

```text

│                                   V1.2.5                                     │
├──────────────────────┬──────────────────────┬──────────────────────┬─────────┤

│ Expansion & Direct   │ Suppression & Role   │ Lineup Horizon       │ Chip    │
│ Lineup Search        │ Transfer Hurdles     │ (Rolling 3-GW Window)│ Weights │
├──────────────────────┼──────────────────────┼──────────────────────┼─────────┤
│ • max_results=50/100 │ • GKP hurdle 1.5x    │ • H=3 rolling window │ • BB:   │
│ • Direct lineup-rank │ • Ban non-injury GK  │ • γ=0.75 discount    │   w=1.00│
│   in candidate pool  │   transfers          │ • Anti-zigzagging    │ • FH:   │
│ • Starter upgrade tag│ • Free up outfield FT│ • Core premium hold  │   w=0.05│
└──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┴─────────┘
```

### Pillar 1: Candidate Pool Expansion & Direct Lineup Scoring
1. **Candidate Pool Expansion:** Expand `max_results` in `solve_transfers` calls from `5` to `50` (or `100`), ensuring that high-ceiling starting XI upgrades are preserved for lineup re-scoring.
2. **Native Lineup Evaluation (Optional Direct Path):** In `solve_transfers`, add support for evaluating candidate swaps directly using the asymmetric lineup objective:
   $$\text{Objective}(S_{\text{cand}}) = \text{LineupXP}(S_{\text{cand}}) + w_{\text{bench}} \cdot \text{BenchXP}(S_{\text{cand}})$$
   so that candidate ranking natively aligns with matchday starting XI impact.

### Pillar 2: Goalkeeper Churn Suppression & Role-Specific Transfer Hurdles
1. **Position-Specific Net Gain Hurdles:**
   $$\text{Hurdle}(p) = \begin{cases} 
   1.50 \text{ pts}, & \text{if position is GOALKEEPER} \\
   0.50 \text{ pts}, & \text{if position is OUTFIELD (DEF/MID/FWD)}
   \end{cases}$$
2. **Goalkeeper Playing Security Invariant:**
   A goalkeeper transfer is rejected unless:
   * The current squad goalkeeper has $P(\text{play}) < 0.50$ (injury, benching, departure); OR
   * The prospective replacement offers a projected 3-gameweek net gain $\ge 3.0$ points.
3. **Target Invariant:** Enforce $\le 1\text{–}2$ goalkeeper transfers per 38-gameweek season, saving $3\text{–}4$ transfers for explosive midfield and forward rotations.

### Pillar 3: Multi-Gameweek Discounted Lineup Horizon ($H=3, \gamma=0.75$)
Rather than evaluating transfers on next week's points alone ($H=1$):
$$\Delta xP_{\text{lineup}} = \text{LineupXP}_{GW}(S_{\text{new}}) - \text{LineupXP}_{GW}(S_{\text{old}})$$
Evaluate rolling discounted lineup returns over $H=3$ gameweeks:
$$\Delta xP_{\text{rolling}} = \sum_{t=0}^{2} \gamma^t \cdot \left( \text{LineupXP}_{GW+t}(S_{\text{new}}) - \text{LineupXP}_{GW+t}(S_{\text{old}}) \right) - \text{HitCost}$$
where $\gamma = 0.75$.
* **Benefits:**
  * Proactively captures multi-week fixture swings (e.g. green fixture runs, Double Gameweeks in GW34/GW37).
  * Suppresses panic sales of elite assets (e.g. Haaland, Saka) during 1-week minor knocks or blank gameweeks.
  * Eliminates the costly GW20–GW23 Haaland/Salah/Richarlison churn.

### Pillar 4: Double Gameweek & Blank Gameweek Awareness
Incorporate fixture count weighting into multi-horizon projections:
$$\text{FixtureDensity}(p, t) = \text{Fixtures}(p, GW+t)$$
When evaluating 3-GW rolling lineup returns, players playing 2 fixtures in a double gameweek naturally reflect higher projected point totals, enabling V1.2.5 to automatically load up on DGW assets (e.g. White and Tarkowski in GW34) without requiring manual heuristics.

### Pillar 5: Dynamic Chip-Aware Bench Weighting
Dynamically adapt the `bench_weight` parameter based on the operational context:
* **Standard Gameweeks:** `bench_weight = 0.15` (preserves starting XI budget concentration).
* **Free Hit Gameweeks:** `bench_weight = 0.05` (concentrates the entire £100m budget into the 11 on-pitch starters for a single matchday).
* **Pre-Bench Boost Window (1–2 GWs prior to BB deployment):** `bench_weight = 0.60` (allows free transfers to rotate budget into playing bench enablers).
* **Bench Boost Gameweek:** `bench_weight = 1.00` (evaluates all 15 players equally, since all 15 score points).
